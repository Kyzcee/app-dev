# Favorite Series all time: Star Trek Deep Space 9 

**Star Trek** is a spin-off of Star trek: The next generation. Set on a space station orbiting the planet bajor. Commander Sisko (The first black American lead actor in Star trek Franchise), with his crew, must fight off rival alien species who want control of Deep Space Nine because of it's strategic position close to an important wormhole.
